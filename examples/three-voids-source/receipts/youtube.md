## Description

A painting of a woman facing a huge black circle. The oldest anarchy server in Minecraft. An empty AI prompt that says "What should we work on?". I think they are the same picture, and I think a lot of us are about to be standing in front of it.

The painting is "Art Block" (2022) by Daniel Conway. I found it rebuilt as map art in the 2b2t museum.
