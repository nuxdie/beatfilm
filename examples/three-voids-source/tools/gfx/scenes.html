What software would you build, if you could build anything?
Deciding how to answer a preferences question›

Probably a tool that shows where any claim comes from. You highlight a sentence in an article, a paper, or a chat with me, and it unfolds the chain behind it: the original study or source, how the claim changed as it got repeated, what the strongest counter-evidence is, and how much weight the whole thing can actually bear.

I’d pick that because most of what people believe arrives third-hand, and checking it takes hours. Make it take seconds, and a lot of confusion and bad arguments never get started. It’s also a hard, fun problem: part search, part reasoning, and part interface design to show uncertainty without overwhelming anyone.

My runner-up is a simulator for everyday decisions, like “what happens to my finances and free time if I take this job?”, which you could poke at like a spreadsheet, but in plain language.
